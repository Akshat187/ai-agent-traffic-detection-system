Hidden gems in Lisbon — what are yours?

I'm spending three weeks in Lisbon in October and I've already done the usual spots. Looking specifically for neighbourhood restaurants that locals actually go to, quiet viewpoints away from Miradouro de Santa Luzia, and any day trips that aren't on every travel blog.

I lived there for two years. Avoid Alfama for dinner — it's all tourist trap. Head to Mouraria instead. Tasca do Chico is excellent but book weeks ahead. For viewpoints, Miradouro da Graça is stunning and almost never crowded in the mornings. For day trips: Setúbal peninsula, not Sintra.

How do you store fresh herbs without them dying in two days?

Every week I buy a big bunch of basil and flat-leaf parsley from the market and within two days they're wilting in the fridge. I've tried damp paper towels, sealed bags, everything. What actually works for you?

Stand them in a glass of water on the counter like flowers — don't refrigerate basil at all, it goes black in the cold. For parsley and coriander, jar of water, loose plastic bag over the top, fridge. Changes the water every two days. They last two weeks easily this way.

Not asking for "books you liked". I mean books where you finished them and your understanding of something fundamental shifted. What's yours?

The simplest meal you reliably cook when you have no energy

Late nights, long weeks. What's the one thing you can make in 15 minutes that still feels like a proper meal? I'll start: pasta aglio e olio — six ingredients, twenty minutes, genuinely good.

Fried rice. Use yesterday's rice (essential — fresh rice is too wet), whatever vegetables are wilting in the fridge, an egg, soy sauce, sesame oil. Ten minutes and better than most takeaways.
